# Gut Feeling mascots

**Ember is the primary mascot.** Mellow and Float are retained alternate concepts.

Asset inventory:

- **Mellow**: the completed minimal hooded mascot, hugging its tummy. Standalone illustration: `mellow-v1.png`. Editable transparent light, dark, and automatic SVGs live in `../svg/`.
- **Ember**: the curled plum creature embracing its glowing orange tummy. Standalone illustration: `ember-v1.png`.
- **Float**: the floating lavender-peach organism with a luminous tummy and two companion droplets. Standalone illustration: `float-v1.png`.

Ember and Float were selected from concepts 01 and 02 of `../concepts/inner-glow-v2.png`. Glowhug remains an unselected exploration.

All three PNG illustrations have opaque cream backgrounds. Editable transparent SVG variants for all three mascots are in `../svg/`, with light, dark, and automatic theme options. Ember and Float SVGs are simplified gradient-based interpretations, not exact reproductions of the raster textures. The adjacent prompt files record the original built-in imagegen generation instructions.
